# Financial Management Web App - User Manual

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to the Financial Management Web App. This tool is designed to help the accounts department digitally manage income, expenses, and generate reports. The application is secure, responsive (works on mobile and desktop), and tailored for Nigerian Naira (₦).

---

## 2. Accessing the System
### Login
1.  Open your web browser and navigate to the application URL (e.g., `http://127.0.0.1:8000/`).
2.  You will be redirected to the **Login Page**.
3.  Enter your **Username** and **Password**.
4.  Click **Sign In**.

> **Default Admin Credentials**:
> *   **Username**: `admin`
> *   **Password**: `admin123`

---

## 3. The Dashboard
Upon logging in, you will see the **Dashboard**. This provides an instant overview of the company's financial health.

*   **Summary Cards**:
    *   **Total Income**: Sum of all money received (Green).
    *   **Total Expenses**: Sum of all money spent (Red).
    *   **Net Balance**: The difference between Income and Expenses.
*   **Charts**: A visual breakdown of Income vs. Expenses.
*   **Recent Transactions**: A quick list of the last 5 records added.

---

## 4. Managing Transactions
To add, view, or edit financial records, click on **Transactions** in the sidebar (or menu on mobile).

### Adding a New Record
1.  Click the blue **+ Add New** button.
2.  Fill in the form:
    *   **Type**: Select `Income` or `Expense`.
    *   **Category**: Enter a category (e.g., "Salary", "Sales", "Logistics").
    *   **Amount**: Enter the amount in Naira.
    *   **Date**: Select the date of the transaction.
    *   **Description**: (Optional) Add details about the transaction.
3.  Click **Save Record**.

### Editing a Record
1.  In the Transactions list, find the record you want to change.
2.  Click the **Yellow Edit Button** (Pencil icon).
3.  Modify the details and click **Save**.

### Deleting a Record
1.  In the Transactions list, click the **Red Delete Button** (Trash icon).
2.  Confirm the deletion when prompted.

---

## 5. Generating Reports
You can export your financial data for offline use or reporting.

1.  Navigate to the **Transactions** page.
2.  Click **Export Excel** (Green button).
3.  The system will generate a `.xlsx` file containing all transactions, which will automatically download to your device.


---

## 6. Payslips
You can generate and print monthly payslips for employees.

1.  Click **Payslips** in the sidebar.
2.  Click **Generate New**.
3.  Enter Employee Name, Month, Basic Salary, Allowances, and Deductions.
4.  Click **Submit**.
5.  In the list view, click **Download PDF** to get a printable version.

---

## 7. Invoices
Manage client invoices and track payments.

1.  Click **Invoices** in the sidebar.
2.  Click **Create New**.
3.  Fill in the client details and amount.
4.  Set the Status to **Unpaid** or **Paid**.
5.  Click **Submit**.
6.  Click **Download PDF** to generate a professional invoice for the client.

---

## 8. Mobile Usage
The application is fully responsive.
*   On **Desktop**, the navigation sidebar is fixed to the left.
*   On **Mobile/Tablet**, the sidebar moves to the top of the screen to maximize space for your data. You can scroll down to view charts and tables.
